name: Recent article curation
description: Nationwide relevance, publication-date checks, and source-integrity rules for healthcare article selections.
---

Select recent healthcare stories with broad U.S. relevance, without presenting them as an independently verified most-read ranking.

**Why:** The user requested 50 recent articles most Americans would likely read, but no readership analytics or common ranking metric was supplied.

**How to apply:** Balance consumer health, costs and coverage, medications, family health, mental health, and aging. Label the selection as curated and dated, not a continuously updated feed unless automatic refresh is actually implemented.

Feed position is not evidence of freshness. Keep publisher publication dates separate from source-check dates and clinical review.

**Why:** A current publisher health feed contained articles originally published in 2021 and 2024, as well as duplicate article/video versions. Syndicated stories also appeared under multiple publishers.

**How to apply:** Verify date provenance and remove duplicates and old recirculated content. Preserve distinctions between research findings, expert explainers, policy proposals, and established medical recommendations. Use original short briefs with full-source links, not copies of copyrighted articles.

Keep verified source metadata in recoverable workspace files rather than only temporary files or execution-session variables.

**Why:** During this curation, an environment restart cleared temporary research files and execution state while tracked workspace edits remained, requiring the verified metadata to be rebuilt.

**How to apply:** Save source references promptly and use stable headlines or URLs for repeatable lookups, not positions in a changing feed.
