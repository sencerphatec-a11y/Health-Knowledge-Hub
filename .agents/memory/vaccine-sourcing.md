---
name: Vaccine source authority
description: How to resolve conflicts between CDC schedule status, calendar years, and older vaccine fact sheets.
---

Use CDC's explicit current-schedule designation and current product-specific notes, not the calendar year or a VIS publication year, to identify vaccination recommendations.

**Why:** In October 2026, CDC identified reinstated July 2025 schedules as current, with an adult RSV amendment. Some current-index VIS documents still contained older seasonal dosing instructions. These sources serve different purposes and update independently.

**How to apply:** Check the live schedule-status notice and clinical notes before updating schedule years, doses, or age indications. Keep fact-sheet benefits, precautions, risks, and emergency information separate from current dosing guidance. A retrieval date is not a medical-review date; link full original documents and make summary limitations explicit.
