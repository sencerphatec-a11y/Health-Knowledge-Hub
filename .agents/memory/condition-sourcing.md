---
name: Condition sourcing
description: Scope and source-integrity decisions for the 50-condition library.
---

The user requested 50 conditions affecting Americans' everyday lives. Present them as a curated, alphabetical set, not an official numerical prevalence ranking.

**Why:** There is no single defensible ranking across acute illnesses, chronic diagnoses, symptoms, and different age groups without a specified population and metric.

**How to apply:** Retain broad everyday-health coverage across body systems and keep selection language honest. Ask for the population and ranking metric if a future request needs numerical rankings.

Source summaries must preserve paragraph/list order and table semantics. Some MedlinePlus XML topics have no full summary; do not silently invent one. Clearly distinguish source snapshot dates from clinical review.

**Why:** Table-blind extraction omitted blood-pressure categories and flu symptom comparisons, while a seasonal-allergy topic had no summary despite being present in the topic index.

**How to apply:** Validate imported content against the source, including tables. Use a relevant documented source where summaries are absent, and attribute original material and dates accurately.
