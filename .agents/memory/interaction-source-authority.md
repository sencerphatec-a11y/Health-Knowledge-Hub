---
name: Interaction source authority
description: Why replacing an interaction-checker provider requires checking its definitions, not just changing links.
---

Verify interaction categories and their explanations against the selected checker's own current documentation. Do not carry one provider's severity model into another provider's resource page.

**Why:** The requested replacement of Drugs.com with Medscape required different category definitions and clinical action language. A name-and-link replacement alone would have misrepresented the new source.

**How to apply:** When replacing or refreshing an interaction resource, compare its current category descriptions and interface labels with the site's summaries, metadata, and citations. Describe provider-specific categories as educational information, not personalized safety clearance.
