Vaccine Educational FAQ

Are vaccine ingredients safe?

Yes. Vaccine ingredients are thoroughly tested for safety. Micro-quantities of salts, sugars, and stabilizers ensure potency and sterility.

What common side effects should I expect?

Mild injection site soreness, low-grade fever, or fatigue lasting 24-48 hours indicate your body is successfully building immune defense.

Why are booster doses required?

Immunity against certain pathogens wanes over time or requires updating due to mutating viral strains (such as seasonal influenza).
